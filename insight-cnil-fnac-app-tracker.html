CNIL fines Fnac €7m — app tracker without consent

Vishal Akshintala · 2026-11-02

You browsed a shopping app. A tracker inside it reported what you looked at, without your consent.

That is what France's data protection authority, the CNIL, found in the Fnac app. Its decision of 22 October 2026, published on 29 October, fines two Fnac Darty companies €7m in total. The tracker sent a Fnac-assigned identifier and the products a user viewed to a third-party domain, to personalise recommendations and to train the recommendation algorithm. The CNIL held that neither purpose was exempt, so both needed prior consent under Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act. The case started with a complaint from noyb.

What I take from it as someone who reviews products: the consent screen is only as good as the code behind it. Third-party SDKs and APIs can start before the user has chosen anything, and "recommendations" or "model training" sound harmless until a regulator calls them targeting. The last slide has four questions worth asking before your next release.

€7m in fines for an app tracker that ran without consent.

  • Fnac Darty Participations et Services: €6m
  • Fnac Direct: €1m
  • Not fixed within a month: €10,000 a day

What the tracker did:

  • sent a user identifier assigned by Fnac to a third-party domain;
  • sent browsing activity, such as the products viewed;
  • fed personalised recommendations and the training of the recommendation algorithm.
  • CNIL, decision SAN-2026-015

Why consent was needed

  • Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act covers cookies and other trackers, including those in a mobile app.
  • The CNIL found that personalised recommendations and algorithm training were not exempt, so they needed prior consent.
  • The case began with a complaint from noyb. The CNIL inspected the app in March 2024.

Four questions to ask

  • Which SDKs and APIs run before the user makes a choice?
  • Does refusing consent stop every tracker, including third-party ones?
  • Are recommendations and model training listed as purposes that need consent?
  • Can you show when and how each user's consent was collected?
