EN 18286 — first EU AI Act harmonised standard

Vishal Akshintala · 2026-10-27

The EU AI Act now has its first harmonised standard, and it is about quality management.

On 14 October 2026, Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2274 was published in the Official Journal, and the Commission announced it on 19 October. It lists one standard: EN 18286:2026, a quality management system for EU AI Act purposes.

Why should a founder or CTO care? Article 40 of the EU AI Act says that following a harmonised standard published in the Official Journal gives a presumption of conformity with the requirements it covers. It remains voluntary, but I see it as the clearest route the law offers.

The question I hear most is whether ISO/IEC 42001 already covers this. It is a valuable AI management system standard, and in my experience much of the groundwork carries over. But it is not the standard listed in the decision, so on its own it does not give that presumption. EN 18286 is built around Article 17 and the product.
