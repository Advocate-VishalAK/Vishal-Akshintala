EU AI Act timeline after the AI Omnibus

Vishal Akshintala · 2026-11-16

The EU AI Act's dates moved this summer. If your compliance plan still uses the old ones, here is the new timeline.

The AI Omnibus, which amends the AI Act, entered into force on 27 July 2026. According to the European Commission, the rules for high-risk AI in sensitive use cases (Annex III) now apply from 2 December 2027, and for AI built into regulated products such as lifts or toys (Annex I) from 2 August 2028. It also added a ninth prohibited practice: AI that generates non-consensual intimate content or child sexual abuse material, from December 2026.

What did not move matters as much. Prohibitions and AI literacy have applied since February 2025, rules for general-purpose AI models since August 2025, and most of the Act, including the transparency rules, since August 2026.

My view: the later high-risk dates are time to prepare, not a pause. Classifying a product and building its documentation rarely happens in a quarter.

What applies, from when

What changed:

  • high-risk rules for sensitive use cases (Annex III) now apply from 2 December 2027;
  • high-risk rules for AI built into products such as lifts or toys (Annex I) apply from 2 August 2028;
  • a ninth prohibited practice: AI that generates non-consensual intimate content or child sexual abuse material, from December 2026.
  • European Commission, AI Act page

What did not move

  • The transparency rules apply from August 2026: AI-generated content must be identifiable, deepfakes labelled, and chatbot users told they are talking to a machine.
  • Since 2 August 2026, the AI Office and national authorities are responsible for enforcing the Act.

What high-risk systems will need

  • Risk management
  • High-quality datasets and logging
  • Documentation and information for deployers
  • Human oversight
  • Robustness, cybersecurity and accuracy
  • European Commission, AI Act page

Time to prepare, not a pause

  • A later deadline moves the date. It does not shrink the work.
  • If your product might be high-risk, the extra months are the time to classify it and start the documentation.
  • Prohibitions, AI literacy and transparency already apply, so those are worth checking first.
