Useful resources — while you wait to connect

National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

File a complaint directly at cybercrime.gov.in, or call the national helpline 1930 — particularly urgent for financial fraud, where speed affects whether funds can be frozen.

CERT-In Incident Reporting

Organizations required to report cybersecurity incidents within the six-hour window under CERT-In's 2022 directions can do so at cert-in.org.in.

If you have been accused in a cybercrime matter

Do not respond to police or platform notices without legal review first. Preserve your own device and account records rather than deleting anything, even if you believe it is irrelevant — this is often misread later as evidence tampering.
