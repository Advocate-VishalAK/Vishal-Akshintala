ICO reprimands — Password1 test account

Vishal Akshintala · 2026-10-31

One forgotten test account, one weak password, and the data of 3,300 people, most of them children.

On 20 October 2026, the UK's data protection regulator, the ICO, reprimanded Ogi Networks and Data Cymru under the UK GDPR. Attackers got in through a test account created in January 2012 that still used the password "Password1". The ransomware attack on 1 November 2024 encrypted 182.7GB of data, and 42GB was taken.

What I find most useful here is that both organisations were found at fault. The IT provider created the account and did not regularly audit the accounts it had set up. The client controlled the network but had no contract with the mandatory data protection terms, and no clear split of who was responsible for what.
