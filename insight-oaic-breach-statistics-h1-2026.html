Breach response

OAIC breach statistics Jan–Jun 2026

Vishal Akshintala · 2026-10-26

Australia's breach numbers barely moved in six months. The size of the breaches did.

On 26 October 2026, the OAIC published its Notifiable Data Breach statistics for January to June 2026: 667 notifications, against 662 in the previous period. But breaches affecting more than a million people went from 4 to 11.

Health service providers notified the most breaches (149), and malicious or criminal attacks remain the leading cause. One encouraging figure: more than half of the breaches were identified within 10 days.

For anyone running a breach response in Australia, the Privacy Act 1988 sets the clock: take all reasonable steps to assess a suspected breach within 30 days, then notify the OAIC and affected people as soon as practicable if it is eligible. The decision about who makes that call is best settled before an incident, not during one.
